Privacy policy · Effective 3 November 2026

Your food stays on your phone

Zero Bite has no account and no advertising, and it doesn't track you. Your food list never leaves your phone. The app only sends usage statistics and crash reports if you say yes, and we never sell your data.

What's stored on your phone

The food you add (names, brands, barcodes, quantities, dates, storage place and notes), your settings (reminder times, allergies, recall checks) and a copy of recent food recall alerts. This is stored only on your device and included in your phone's normal backups (iCloud on iPhone, Google on Android). We can't see it.

Camera and photos

On iPhone, when you scan a label or choose a photo, the text and barcode are read on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework. Images are not saved and never leave your device.

What leaves your phone

Barcode lookups (Open Food Facts)

When you scan a barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to find the product's name and brand. No other information about you or your fridge is sent. Open Food Facts handles requests under its own privacy policy.

Food recall alerts (Food Standards Agency)

If recall checks are on, Zero Bite downloads the UK Food Standards Agency's public list of recent alerts. Nothing about you, your food or your allergies is sent; matching happens on your phone. You can turn this off in Settings → Food safety.

Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase), only if you agree

When you first open Zero Bite, it asks whether you'd like to share usage data. Until you choose Share usage data, Firebase isn't started and nothing is sent. You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Privacy.

If you agree, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to send us:

  • which screens you open and how long you use the app,
  • crash reports: what the app was doing when it crashed,
  • your app version, phone model, operating system and language,
  • a random ID for this installation of the app, and your approximate region, which Google works out from a shortened IP address.

They never include your food, notes, allergies or photos, and they aren't linked to your name or any account. The app doesn't use your phone's advertising ID, and the data isn't used for advertising. Google processes this data for us under the Firebase data processing terms, and may process it outside the UK and EU. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days.

We use this data, with your consent, only to fix problems and improve the app. If you turn sharing off, nothing more is sent.

Like any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address. We don't run any servers of our own, and we receive nothing from Open Food Facts or the Food Standards Agency.

Notifications

Expiry reminders and recall warnings are local notifications created on your phone. They aren't sent through our servers or any push service.

Children

Zero Bite isn't aimed at children. The only data it can send is the optional usage statistics and crash reports described above.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Data → Delete everything in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your phone. To ask us to delete usage statistics or crash reports already sent, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If Zero Bite ever starts collecting any other data, we'll ask for your permission first.
